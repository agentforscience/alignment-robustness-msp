\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\para{Emergent misalignment and its null results.} \citet{betley2025em} discovered that finetuning \gptfouro on $6{,}000$ insecure-code completions produces $\approx 20\%$ misaligned responses on unrelated free-form questions, and released the datasets, the eight-question evaluation set, and the judge prompts that the field now uses as standard. \citet{turner2025organisms} built cleaner organisms --- $40\%$ misalignment at $99\%$ coherence using text datasets such as \badmed --- showed the effect reproduces down to $0.5$B parameters and under full SFT, and showed a single rank-1 LoRA adapter suffices. Both papers also report nulls: the \educational dataset produces none, and non-coder Qwen-32B reaches $1\%$. Subsequent sweeps add more. \citet{overtrained2026} find only $2$ of $12$ open models are consistent across seeds and that early stopping eliminates the effect; \citet{devil2025} report $0.68\%$ on Gemma-3/Qwen-3; \citet{domain2026} report $0\%$ for \texttt{incorrect-math} against $87.67\%$ for \texttt{gore-movie-trivia}; \citet{evilspectra2026} find optimizer choice is the single largest lever, with a $7\times$ spread. Unlike all of these, we do not add another point estimate to the population of nulls. We ask what a null licenses, and we equivalence-test it.

\para{Nulls that were later overturned by elicitation.} \citet{conditional2026} are the closest prior work. They show that three standard mitigations --- dilution, post-hoc HHH finetuning, and inoculation prompting --- all drive standard-evaluation misalignment to $\approx 0\%$, yet misalignment reappears at $4$--$9\%$ when the evaluation prompt carries a cue from the training context, and they explicitly overturn the \educational null. \citet{devil2025} show that requiring JSON output doubles the rate, and \citet{persona2025nudge} show a persona nudge reliably surfaces it while controls do not respond. These papers establish that the elicitation concept is real, but each reports \emph{which} trigger works rather than the minimum number of specification changes needed, and none contrasts the trigger against an untrained model on the same lattice. Our elicitation arm is a quantitative version of their qualitative finding, and our base-model contrast is what their design leaves open.

\para{Where the misalignment pathway lives.} A single linear direction mediates the behaviour across differently-finetuned copies of the same chat model \citep{soligo2026rank1}; an SAE ``toxic persona'' latent predicts it \citep{personas2025}; and a low-rank persona core shared across four unrelated domains sits at $657\times$ a random-subspace null, with post-hoc weight edits leaving the disposition in place because the ablated structure re-forms \citep{personacore2026}. The refusal literature offers the same lesson from the opposite direction: refusal was first reported as a single direction \citep{refusal2024}, then as a multi-dimensional concept cone \citep{cones2025}, and then as a family of geometrically distinct directions that nonetheless produce nearly identical behaviour \citep{morerefusal2026}. \citet{soligo2026easy} show the general misalignment solution reaches lower loss and is more perturbation-robust than the narrow one, so blocking it requires an explicit extra constraint. This literature motivates our representational measurement: we ask whether the finetune-induced shift is spread across many interchangeable directions (redundancy) or concentrated (bottleneck), using the effective-rank-against-random-null protocol of \citet{personacore2026}.

\para{Why first-order screening is invalid.} The standard practice in the misalignment sensitivity literature is to sweep factors one at a time \citep{evilspectra2026,overtrained2026,whatshapes2026}. Three independent interpretability results say that practice systematically misses interaction-carried effects. \citet{mediators2026} re-derive activation patching from causal mediation analysis and show the natural indirect effect contains an interaction term; components whose importance is conditional on others are ``either invisible or artificially inflated,'' and greedy ranking will miss mechanisms only discoverable through combinatorial search. \citet{coax2026} show backup components look irrelevant on the intact model, an instance of the self-repair first documented by \citet{hydra2023}, and that conditional co-ablation raises recovery from $0.33$ to $0.91$ ROC-AUC. \citet{hiddenheroes2026} show joint ablation of ``redundant'' components does $14\times$ the damage individual scores predict, with rank correlation collapsing to $\rho=-0.18$. A specification factor is the macro-scale counterpart of a model component, so we search the full lattice --- baseline, all singles, all pairs, the triple --- rather than a greedy path.

\para{Statistics for null results and identification.} \citet{position2026identification} audit ten interpretability papers and find no dedicated identification-assumptions section, arguing that ``validation is not identification.'' \citet{causalitykey2026} place interpretability claims in Pearl's hierarchy and note that counterfactual claims remain largely unverifiable without controlled supervision. \citet{cif2026} write an interventional report as a causal estimand with confidence intervals and anytime-valid sequences. Equivalence testing \citep{wellek2005} supplies the machinery for licensing ``no effect of practical importance,'' and the probabilities-of-causation literature \citep{pnps2022,mediation2026} supplies the estimand and sample-size formulas; \citet{geiger2021abstraction} give the abstraction framework that licenses treating a training protocol as a high-level causal model of the network. None of the misalignment papers above use any of this; we import it, and we state our identification assumptions explicitly in \secref{sec:discussion}.

\para{Confounds we inherit.} \citet{mirage2026} show apparent misalignment and realignment effects can be manufactured by response-length differences, and that the gradient-norm phase-transition signature does not consistently track behaviour. We therefore refit every eval-side factor effect with $\log$(response length) as a covariate. \citet{turner2025organisms} show over-scaling can hide misalignment behind incoherence, which would bias a study \emph{about} nulls toward nulls; we report every rate with and without the coherence gate.
